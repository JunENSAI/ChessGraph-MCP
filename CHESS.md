# Chess

In my perspective, Chess belongs to those games called : `Strategic & Analytic Game`.

Chess is a known board game that has $8 \times 8$ dimensions. It's regularly played between two players but it has some variants that can be played by more than $2$ players. In case of two player, there is two sides : **White pieces** and **Black pieces**. In the beginning, each players have $16$ pieces on the board [*if we count distinctly, they have each $6$ pieces*]. The player who plays with the white pieces begin the game. So, often strong players who played this games about decade call this `having the initiative on the game`.
